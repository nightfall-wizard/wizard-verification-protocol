# WVP Risk Model

## Risk Classes

- low
- medium
- high
- extreme

## Risk Areas

- code risk
- build risk
- dependency risk
- security risk
- centralization risk
- developer risk
- documentation risk
- claim inflation risk
- economic reality risk
- operational continuity risk

## WVP-X

WVP-X is assigned when severe contradiction, severe unverifiable claims, severe technical weakness or high manipulation risk is found.
