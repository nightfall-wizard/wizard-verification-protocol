# AUNEYA Legal Boundary

## Status

AUNEYA is non-value protocol research and simulation only.

This repository does not provide legal advice.

AUNEYA must remain outside token, value, custody, payment, investment, financial-service, and crypto-asset-service activity unless a qualified legal review explicitly approves a future change.

## Core legal boundary

AUNEYA does not create, issue, sell, distribute, transfer, custody, broker, advise on, promote, provide, or operate any:

- crypto-asset;
- token;
- financial instrument;
- investment product;
- payment service;
- custody service;
- exchange service;
- transfer service;
- brokerage service;
- advisory service;
- portfolio-management service;
- staking service;
- mining-reward service;
- yield product;
- public offer;
- admission-to-trading process;
- market-value communication.

## Germany / EU jurisdiction note

The project maintainer operates from a Germany/EU context.

Before any token, reward, transferability, wallet, custody, payment, listing, sale, market-value, public-offer, or crypto-asset-service communication is introduced, qualified Germany/EU legal review is required.

## No-value rule

AUNEYA currently creates no token, no transferable value, no claim to market value, no yield, no return, no sale, no staking, no mining reward, no investment product, and no financial outcome.

## No-service rule

AUNEYA does not provide:

- custody;
- transfer of crypto-assets;
- operation of a trading platform;
- exchange of crypto-assets;
- execution of orders;
- placement of crypto-assets;
- reception or transmission of orders;
- advice on crypto-assets;
- portfolio management;
- payment services.

## Communication rule

AUNEYA may be described only as:

- protocol research;
- architecture research;
- simulation design;
- non-value witness-network exploration;
- provable-web research;
- phone-first witness architecture research.

AUNEYA must not be described as:

- an investment;
- a token opportunity;
- a launch;
- an airdrop;
- a reward network;
- a yield system;
- a staking product;
- a market-value project;
- a financial product;
- a crypto-asset service;
- a payment network;
- a custody system.

## Hard legal stop

Any future change involving token, reward, yield, payment, custody, transferability, listing, sale, market value, financial outcome, public offering, user funds, wallets, private keys, or regulated service language is blocked until qualified legal review is completed and recorded.

## Non-claims

This repository does not claim:

- legal compliance;
- regulatory approval;
- BaFin approval;
- MiCAR compliance;
- securities-law compliance;
- payment-law compliance;
- tax treatment;
- investment suitability;
- custody safety;
- audit status;
- financial-service status.
