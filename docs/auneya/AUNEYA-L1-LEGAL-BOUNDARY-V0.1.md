# AUNEYA L1 Legal Boundary v0.1

AUNEYA is documented as a permissionless Layer One research implementation and phone-first witness network.

AUNEYA is intended to run as protocol infrastructure, not as an investment product.

This document defines the legal communication boundary for any future AUNEYA Layer One implementation.

## Status

- Research boundary
- Simulation boundary
- Non-value communication boundary
- No public token offer
- No trading-admission request
- No market-value communication

This document is not legal advice. Any public offer, trading admission, sale, listing, transferable token activation or market-value communication requires separate legal review before execution.

## Explicit boundaries

AUNEYA must not be presented as any of the following:

1. No token-sale framing.
2. No presale framing.
3. No allocation-sale framing.
4. No investment-product framing.
5. No profit-opportunity framing.
6. No return, yield or appreciation-promise framing.
7. No stablecoin framing.
8. No asset-referenced-token framing.
9. No exchange-service framing.
10. No broker-service framing.
11. No custody-service framing for third-party assets.
12. No financial-advisory-service framing.
13. No third-party transfer-service framing.

## Permitted research framing

AUNEYA may be described as:

1. A permissionless Layer One research implementation.
2. A phone-first witness network.
3. A protocol-level verification and witness architecture.
4. A non-value simulation until separate legal review says otherwise.
5. An open-source protocol experiment with no sale, no profit promise and no market-value claim.

## Coin boundary

If AUNEYA later contains a native protocol coin, the boundary is:

1. No token sale.
2. No presale.
3. No allocation sale.
4. No market-value claim.
5. No profit, return, yield or appreciation claim.
6. No stablecoin peg.
7. No issuer redemption claim.
8. No custody of third-party assets.
9. No exchange, broker, advice or transfer service.
10. Coins, if any, may only arise from protocol-defined mining, validation, witness or consensus mechanics after separate legal review.

## Hard stop triggers

The project must stop and require legal review before any of the following:

1. Public token offer.
2. Public sale.
3. Private allocation sale.
4. Trading-admission request.
5. Exchange listing request.
6. Market-value communication.
7. Transferability activation.
8. Reward communication with economic framing.
9. Stablecoin or asset-reference design.
10. Custody, exchange, broker, advice or third-party transfer service.

## No-conflict rule

If another AUNEYA document appears to conflict with this boundary, the stricter non-value interpretation controls until legal review approves a change.

## Implementation rule

Every future AUNEYA technical step must remain compatible with this boundary unless a separate legal review explicitly authorizes a different path.
