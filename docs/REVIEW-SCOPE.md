
# WVP Review Scope

## Primary scope

WVP review focuses on evidence quality.

The review scope includes:

- whether evidence exists
- whether evidence is traceable
- whether generated reports are reproducible
- whether checkers are understandable
- whether limitations are visible
- whether public outputs are sanitized
- whether non-audit language is preserved

## Explicit exclusions

The review scope excludes:

- proving Nightfall is safe
- proving consensus correctness
- proving cryptographic soundness
- proving absence of inflation bugs
- proving absence of theft bugs
- proving real-world anonymity
- giving legal advice
- giving investment advice

## Security-sensitive material

Security-sensitive observations must be handled privately.

Public review should use sanitized summaries only.

## Boundary

Review scope controls expectations. It does not convert WVP into an audit.
