# WVP Conformance Scoring Method

WVP-SEC-009 scores evidence completeness.

The score measures whether expected WVP artifacts exist
and are machine-readable.

The score does not measure:

- cryptographic correctness
- consensus correctness
- implementation safety
- exploitability
- market value
- audit quality
- independent review quality

## Status levels

PASS:
All required artifacts for that requirement are present.

WARN:
Some required artifacts are present, but coverage is incomplete.

FAIL:
Most required artifacts are missing.

## Grade

A:
90 percent or higher.

B:
75 to 89.99 percent.

C:
60 to 74.99 percent.

D:
45 to 59.99 percent.

F:
Below 45 percent.

## Non-audit rule

A WVP conformance score must never be described as an audit.
